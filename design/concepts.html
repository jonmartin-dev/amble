Four ways Almanac could look

Each concept shows the same Tuesday: the morning briefing, your day list, the week's load with a gap suggestion, and a grocery list made from the Groceries template. Swipe each row sideways to see all four screens.

A · Grove B · Field Notes C · Tide D · Blocks
Phones

Option A

Grove

Material 3 Expressive played straight. It feels like a Pixel app from the first tap, and it's the easiest path to a native Android version later.

  • TypeGoogle Sans Flex, the face Android itself uses
  • ColorTonal greens with warm amber for busy days, generated the way Material You does it
  • LoadA filling meter and rounded bars, with amber where days run heavy
  • FeelFloating pill toolbar, big rounded cards, springy motion

Option B

Field Notes

Leans into the name. Each day reads like a page from an almanac: sunrise and sunset, a load forecast in weather words, and tasks set like ledger entries.

  • TypeLiterata serif for reading, IBM Plex Mono for times and estimates
  • ColorInk blue on cool paper with a marigold highlight. Dark mode is a night edition.
  • LoadA forecast: Fair, Busy, Stormy, drawn as print hatching that gets denser
  • FeelCalm and printed rather than app-like. The most distinctive, least Android-standard.

Option C

Tide

Load as water. Each day is a glass that fills as plans and calendar events land, so you can see at a glance where there's room.

  • TypeUrbanist, a soft geometric sans with heavy numerals
  • ColorDeep sea teal with a sand accent. Designed dark-first, so try the Dark toggle.
  • LoadWater level with a gently drifting wave, the most glanceable of the four
  • FeelSoft and quiet, with one playful visual idea carrying the whole app

Option D

Blocks

Expressive turned all the way up. Every group gets a loud color block, numbers are huge, and Material's playful shapes show up as badges.

  • TypeBricolage Grotesque for big numbers, Figtree for everything else
  • ColorSaturated group colors on plain white or true black
  • LoadA sunny-shaped badge with the day's total, and chunky bars with the gap day lit up
  • FeelEnergetic and fun. Fastest to scan, loudest of the four.

How to pick: tell me a letter, or mix and match, for example Grove's layout with Field Notes' forecast words, or Tide's water bars inside Grove. Everything shown is sample data for a made-up Tuesday.
